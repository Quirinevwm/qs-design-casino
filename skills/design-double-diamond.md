# Design Double Diamond

The Double Diamond provides a shared mental model for when to open up and when to decide. In AI-accelerated design cycles, it keeps teams honest: explore widely, then converge with intent. It clarifies where we are in the flow, what we owe next, and what "done" should feel like.

## The Model

Created at the British Design Council in the early 2000s and published in 2005, led by Richard Eisermann and a multidisciplinary team. The model makes the invisible rhythm of design visible so teams can work with more clarity and confidence.

**Four stages:**
1. **Discover** (diverge) - Explore the problem space widely
2. **Define** (converge) - Synthesize and decide what matters
3. **Validate** (diverge) - Test hypotheses and explore solutions
4. **Deliver** (converge) - Build and ship with quality

**Human evaluation at convergence points**: The power of the Double Diamond appears when human judgment and AI work together to accelerate convergence. AI gives rapid exploration; humans bring decisive convergence that makes work coherent, grounded, and shippable.

---

## Stage 1: Discover (Diverge)

**Goal**: Explore the problem space widely. Open up possibilities.

**AI as perspective expander**: AI pulls in competitor signals, fresh patterns, and references you might not find in your usual circles. It widens the inspiration funnel and gives high-resolution context.

### How AI helps:
- Sweep through broad spaces at speed
- Pull in adjacent patterns, competitor moves, weak signals
- Generate multiple options and references
- Audit what surrounds the problem
- Surface patterns across large datasets

### Human role:
- Define what to explore
- Ask the right questions
- Recognize meaningful patterns
- Maintain curiosity and openness

### Activities:
- User research and interviews
- Market and competitor analysis
- Pattern recognition across domains
- Collecting weak signals and trends
- Gathering diverse perspectives

### Exit criteria:
- Wide range of insights collected
- Problem space mapped
- User needs and pain points identified
- Opportunities surfaced
- Ready to synthesize and decide

---

## Stage 2: Define (Converge)

**Goal**: Synthesize insights and decide what matters. Form clear intent.

**Human judgment is critical**: AI can cluster themes and reveal relationships, but designers bring judgment by interpreting what patterns mean for users, the business, accessibility, and the brand. This is where we shape context, form narrative, and decide which signals carry real weight.

### How AI helps:
- Cluster themes and reveal relationships
- Illuminate patterns (not just volume)
- Draft problem statements for refinement
- Generate hypotheses to test
- Synthesize large volumes of input

### Human role:
- Interpret what patterns mean
- Make decisive choices about focus
- Define the problem clearly
- Set quality principles
- Establish decision boundaries

### Activities:
- Synthesizing research findings
- Defining user jobs to be done (JTBDs)
- Creating problem statements
- Setting quality principles
- Forming testable hypotheses
- Establishing exit criteria

### Exit criteria (clear context accelerates the next diamond):
- Clear problem statement
- Explicit quality principles
- Decision boundaries defined
- Testable hypotheses formed
- Shared understanding across team

**Critical checkpoint**: Human evaluation ensures the problem is framed correctly before moving forward.

---

## Stage 3: Validate (Diverge)

**Goal**: Test hypotheses. Explore solutions through rapid experimentation.

**Multiple parallel diamonds**: AI enables spinning up multiple Double Diamonds in parallel. Form hypotheses quickly and test them at a scale never accessible before.

### How AI helps:
- Generate multiple solution concepts rapidly
- Create variations for testing
- Synthesize feedback patterns
- Identify which directions show promise
- Accelerate iteration cycles

### Human role:
- Design meaningful tests
- Evaluate concept quality
- Interpret user feedback
- Decide what to validate
- Maintain quality standards

### Activities:
- User testing and conversations
- Structured experiments and A/B tests
- Prototyping and iteration
- Gathering qualitative feedback
- Running quantitative tests
- Validating hypotheses against criteria

### Exit criteria:
- Hypotheses confirmed or rejected
- User value validated
- Quality bar verified
- Clear direction chosen
- Ready to build with confidence

---

## Stage 4: Deliver (Converge)

**Goal**: Build and ship. Ensure output meets quality bar.

**Tighten the loops**: This is where loops tighten, alignment strengthens, and solutions prove themselves in real contexts. Assess whether output meets quality standards as you build with engineering.

### How AI helps:
- Generate production-ready code
- Create design specifications
- Draft documentation
- Automate quality checks
- Support implementation consistency

### Human role:
- Ensure quality standards met
- Make final refinement decisions
- Validate against user needs
- Ship with confidence
- Learn from outcomes

### Activities:
- Building with engineering
- Quality reviews and testing
- Refining based on real implementation
- Documentation and handoff
- Shipping and monitoring
- Capturing learnings

### Exit criteria:
- Quality bar met
- User needs satisfied
- Solution shipped
- Team aligned on outcomes
- Learnings captured for next cycle

---

## Working with AI Across the Diamond

### Faster convergence through:
- **Crisp exit criteria**: Clear problem statements, explicit quality principles, and testable hypotheses remove churn
- **Judgment at convergence**: AI explores widely, humans decide with clarity
- **Multiple parallel diamonds**: Test more hypotheses simultaneously
- **Rapid validation**: Get feedback faster, iterate with confidence

### Practical practices:
- Short output-focused standups
- Explicit quality principles alignment
- Diff-based testing of concepts
- Agent skills for behavior analysis and quality reviews
- Shared repos for skills, guidelines, patterns
- Contextual feedback in real surfaces

### Key principle:
**AI accelerates exploration. Human judgment ensures coherent, grounded, shippable work.**

The Double Diamond with AI is not about working faster alone. It is about converging with clarity instead of drifting with volume. It makes teams work sharper, not just faster.

---

## Using This Skill

**When starting a project:**
1. Identify which stage you're in
2. Use AI to accelerate divergence
3. Apply human judgment at convergence
4. Set clear exit criteria before moving forward

**When stuck:**
1. Check if you're trying to converge too early
2. Consider if you've explored widely enough
3. Verify your exit criteria are clear
4. Ensure human judgment is shaping decisions

**When moving fast:**
1. Remember: speed without clarity creates churn
2. Use exit criteria to tighten loops
3. Let AI handle volume, bring judgment to focus
4. Converge with intent, not just data

This skill interprets the model for AI-accelerated design contexts while maintaining human judgment at the center.
