# Amazon Bedrock AgentCore signed-out page: first-impression craft

## Evaluation summary

The page makes a strong initial product impression through a clear hero, prominent primary action, consistent typography, and polished product imagery. Its craft weakens below the fold: repeated text-and-screenshot modules, very large empty intervals, layered navigation, and persistent assistant prompts make the experience feel heavier and less controlled than the hero suggests. The mobile hero adapts well, but the assistant cards obscure the next section and dominate the viewport.

**Patterns worth borrowing**
- Pair a concise promise with one dominant action and one secondary action.
- Use alternating product demonstrations to connect benefits with concrete interfaces.
- Support technical claims with customer examples and a named source.
- Preserve consistent heading, button, and image treatments across sections.

**Anti-patterns to avoid**
- Do not let sales and AI-assistant prompts cover primary page content, especially on mobile.
- Avoid stacking global navigation, product navigation, breadcrumbs, and floating utilities without reducing competing chrome.
- Avoid oversized vertical gaps that break continuity and make the page appear partially loaded.
- Do not repeat nearly identical media modules without enough visual or structural variation.
