    Features

    Bring every agent under control

    Discover, connect and orchestrate agents across your enterprise. Govern how they operate, measure performance and optimize business value.

    Connect supported third-party agent environments, automatically scan for agents and bring them into the watsonx Orchestrate control plane, including agents running in environments such as Amazon Bedrock.

    Intelligently coordinate native and external agents, tools and workflows across the enterprise. Bring complex work together so the right agent or system can act at the right time.

    Explore multi-agent orchestration

    Understand how well agents perform using real user feedback and operational signals. Track response reliability and tool-call success to identify where agents succeed, fail and need improvement.

    Apply consistent access, policy, and oversight as your agent portfolio grows. Govern agents centrally instead of one environment at a time.

    See where agent activity is driving AI consumption with visibility into token usage and LLM calls. Track usage over time to better understand and manage the cost of your agent portfolio.

    Define and apply policies across agents, tools and models to help keep agentic activity within enterprise requirements. Enforce controls for risks such as sensitive or personally identifiable information as work is executed.
